# Spot Kick Legends

A mobile-first penalty shootout tournament game in one HTML file, with no dependencies. Open `index.html` in a phone or desktop browser.

- Choose from 51 national teams. Each has its current squad and a list of historical legends you can sign with coins.
- Play a 16-team knockout cup where every match is a penalty shootout. The bracket is shown between matches.
- Shooting: drag on the goal to aim. The Power and Curve sliders make shots harder to save but widen the accuracy ring.
- Defending: tap one of six zones in the goal to dive.
- Coins come from goals, top-corner finishes, saves, wins, clean sheets and cup wins. Spend them on permanent upgrades (precision, power, bend, keeper reflexes, nerves, scouting, sponsorship) or on legends.
- Each cup you win raises the difficulty tier: rival keepers get better, rival legends start taking kicks, and coin rewards go up.
- Progress is saved in localStorage.
